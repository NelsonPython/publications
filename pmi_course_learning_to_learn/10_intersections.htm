INTERSECTIONS OF HUMAN AND MACHINE LEARNING


Discuss in breakout rooms. Based on what you've learned so far, do you agree or disagree with these observations:
  • Humans process images quickly and efficiently. Machines process numbers quickly and efficiently.

  • Humans communicate via a shared language. Machines communicate via protocols such as XML or JSON. Protocols could be considered a very simple language.

  • Humans schedule communication events called, meetings, by using a calendar. Machines can schedule communication events using scheduling software similar to a calendar.

  • Humans chose when to be available to listen. Machines can listen all the time. A programmer can open a port or publish an API (Application Programming Interface).

  • Humans retain knowledge while they use it. Machines retain data while they have electricity. This data can be organized to reflect knowledge.

  • Humans learn through experience and machines learn through statistical analysis. Both methods have bias: Humans perceive situations through the lens of their culture. Machines are agnostic but humans perceive what machines learn through the lens of their culture.
