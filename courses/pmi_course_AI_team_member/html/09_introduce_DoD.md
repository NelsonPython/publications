### [5 AI Ethical Principles](https://www.defense.gov/Watch/Video/videoid/740566)
<p align="center"><b>established by the USA DoD in 2020</b></p>

#### Responsible
Humans (DOD personnel) are responsible for the development, deployment, and use of AI

#### Traceable
Humans (relevant personnel) will possess an appropriate understanding of the technology, development processes, and operational methods applicable to AI. AI must have auditable methodologies, data sources, and design procedures and documentation

#### Reliable
AI must have well-defined uses.
The safety, security, and effectiveness of AI will be subject to testing across their entire life cycle

#### Governable
AI will possess the ability to detect and avoid unintended consequences. Humans will have the tools to deactivate AI that demonstrates unintended behavior
